Joker's Run privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026. Publisher: Randroid LLC.

Joker's Run for Samsung Smart TV works offline. The game does not collect or share personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, purchases, analytics, tracking, or server uploads.

Saved on your TV

Your best score and settings are saved locally in the app's private web storage. They are not sent to us. Removing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes these records, subject to your TV's storage behavior.

Controls and audio

Remote and gamepad input is used locally to control the game. Controller input is not recorded or transmitted. The game does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or access to your personal files. Music and effects are generated on your TV. Recorded radio clips and captions are included in the app; no speech service receives your audio or text.

Support

For support or privacy questions, email support@toyboxes.app. If you contact us, we use the information you send to respond. Samsung's TV platform and app store operate under Samsung's own privacy policies.
