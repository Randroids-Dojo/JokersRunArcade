Joker's Run Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026. Publisher: Randroid LLC.

The Joker's Run Android game works offline. Randroid LLC does not collect or share personal data through the game. It contains no accounts, advertising, purchases, analytics, tracking, or server uploads.

Data on your device

Your best score and game settings are saved in the game's private storage. This data stays on your device; the Android game disables cloud backup and device transfer. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to erase this data.

Controls and sound

Optional tilt steering uses device orientation locally to control your aircraft. Motion readings are not saved or transmitted. Optional haptics use Android's vibration permission. The game does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or shared-storage access. Music and effects are synthesized on your device. Radio voices are prerecorded clips bundled with the game and played locally; no text or audio is sent to a speech service. Captions remain available when radio voice is off.

Website and support

This policy describes the Android game. Visiting the browser edition or this policy page involves your browser and website hosting provider processing ordinary web requests, such as your IP address. If you email us for support, we use the information you send to respond; do not send information you do not want us to receive.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if the game's data practices change. For privacy or support questions, contact support@toyboxes.app.
